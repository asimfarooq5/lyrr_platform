FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION (FRS)
LYRR: AUDIO AND TEXT READING APPLICATION
1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

LYRR is a mobile, desktop, and web-based application that allows users to access, read, and listen to
digital books in text and/or audio format.
The application shall provide synchronized reading functionality between displayed text and
corresponding audio narration.
The primary objective is to offer a secure digital content distribution platform with multiple free and paid
access models.
2. TARGET PLATFORMS

The application shall be available on:
• Android
• iOS
• Windows Desktop
• Web-based Administration Portal
3. USER ROLES

The following user profiles shall be supported:
3.1 Reader

Functions:
• Account registration
• User login
• Browse the digital library
• Read books
• Listen to audio books
• Manage subscriptions
3.2 Administrator

Functions:
• User management
• Book management
• Subscription management
• Statistics and reporting
• Audio content management
